    About ArWalk

    ArWalk is a small map that finds a walking route and talks you along it. The map is drawn by this page itself on a canvas, with no mapping library. Drag to move, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap the map to drop a pin.

    Every service used is free and needs no key. Map pictures come from Esri (with HERE, Garmin and OpenStreetMap contributors), place search uses Photon from Komoot and walking routes use the OpenStreetMap Germany routing service, so what you search for and the start and end of a route are sent to them. Your live position is used on this device only and is never saved.
