Tools, kept in one place

A shelf of single-file apps. Each one runs entirely in your browser, no accounts, no tracking.

Available tools

  • ArPlan Weekly itinerary, meal planning, and pantry tracker.
  • ArCast A calm, accessible UK weather dashboard with moon phase tracking.
  • ArCanvas A distraction-free drawing experience for children.
  • ArHar A network log (HAR file) inspector.
  • ArCom A zero-dependency comic library and reader for your local collection, with CVD support.
