    About ArTimeline

    ArTimeline makes a simple vertical timeline. You choose the start month and year. The timeline runs to the current month. Each note has a heading and a body, and can sit in one month or in the whole year.

    Save the result as a tagged PDF with selectable text. Your timeline stays in this browser.

    ArTimeline is part of ArTools, a set of single-file tools that work offline.
